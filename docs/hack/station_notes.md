# The ranger station's B1F notes: for editing

B1F was one ranger's post, alone, guarding a post to nowhere. Nobody told them what they guarded. A hum came up
through the floor every night, and they slowly went mad trying to read the torches and furnaces. They never left:
their remains sit slumped against the study's wall, holding the last note.

The five notes are pages from the ranger's log, one from each stage of their stay. Together they give the time to
set on the clock: **4:15 a.m.** Each note carries one hint, and no two hint at the same thing. If you change a hint,
keep the set adding up to one time, or tell me the new time and I'll change the puzzle to match.

**The lore of 4:15:** the station's founders sealed three Pokémon below (the chamber's trio) at a quarter past four
one morning, the night the ground split. They built the clock to that hour, and the trio stirs at that hour every
night: that's the hum. Notes 4 and 5 carry it.

Each `---` is a new text box. A box shows two lines (a third scrolls up in the same box); a line fits about 208
pixels, roughly 32-38 characters. I'll measure and rewrap if needed. Source: `data/maps/RangerStation_B1F/scripts.inc`.
